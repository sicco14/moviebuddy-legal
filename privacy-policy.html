02 Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

When you register, we collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — never in plain text)
  • Timestamp of Terms of Service acceptance
  • Email verification status

2.2 Usage Data

When you use the App, we store:

  • Movie ratings and watchlist entries you create
  • Follow relationships between users
  • Group memberships and activity history
  • Timestamps of your actions within the App

2.3 Technical Data

For push notifications, we store an FCM device token associated with your account. Our server logs record request timestamps, HTTP methods, response codes, and anonymized user identifiers for security and debugging purposes. Logs are rotated after 5 MB.

03 Legal Basis for Processing

Pursuant to GDPR Art. 6:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) — Processing necessary to perform the contract (providing the App service)
  • Art. 6(1)(a) — Your consent, given when accepting these Terms at registration
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — Our legitimate interest in securing and improving the service (server logs)

04 Third-Party Services

4.1 Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications to your device. Your FCM token is transmitted to Google servers. Google may process this data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. Privacy policy

4.2 Resend (Resend Inc.)

We use Resend to send transactional emails (account verification, password reset). Your email address is transmitted to Resend for this purpose. Privacy policy

4.3 The Movie Database (TMDB)

Movie and TV metadata is retrieved from TMDB via their public API. No personal data is transmitted to TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Privacy policy · Terms of Use

4.4 Cloudflare Inc.

Our infrastructure is protected and proxied by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process IP addresses and request metadata for DDoS protection and performance optimization. Data may be processed in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. Privacy policy

05 Data Retention

Your personal data is stored for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently deleted from our systems. Server logs are automatically rotated and do not contain full personal identifiers.

06 Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — delete your account and data at any time via the App
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — request limited processing
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: [email protected]

07 Data Security
