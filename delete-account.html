Delete Your Account

You can delete your MovieBuddy account at any time directly from within the app. This page explains how.

How to delete your account

1
Open the MovieBuddy app Make sure you are logged in to the account you want to delete.
2
Open Settings Tap the Settings icon on the bottom navigator.
3
Tap "Delete Account" Find the Delete Account option and confirm your choice with your password.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data including ratings, watchlist entries, follow relationships, and group memberships will be permanently removed from our systems.

Need help?

If you are unable to access the app or need assistance deleting your account, you can contact us directly and we will delete your account manually within 30 days.

Email: info@moviebuddy.xyz

Please include the email address associated with your account in your request.
